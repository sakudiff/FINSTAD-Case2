\section{Data Acquisition and Preparation}

This section details the empirical data sources, sampling parameters, asset universe selection, and cleaning procedures applied to construct the portfolio dataset for Group 3.

\subsection{Asset Universe and Sampling Specifications}

The portfolio universe comprises seven financial assets spanning three distinct asset classes, alongside the benchmark index and domestic risk-free rate. The sample period covers six full calendar years from January 2, 2020 through December 31, 2025, yielding a balanced panel of 1,509 daily trading observations per asset.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Philippine Equities (3):} PLDT Inc. (TEL), Manila Electric Company (MER), and Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV).
  \item \textbf{International Equities (2):} NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) and Meta Platforms, Inc. (META).
  \item \textbf{Cryptocurrency (1):} Bitcoin (BTC).
  \item \textbf{Benchmark Index:} SPDR S\&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY).
  \item \textbf{Risk-Free Rate:} Philippine 91-Day Treasury Bill benchmark rate.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data Cleaning, Alignment, and Return Computation}

Market price series were programmatically collected via automated Python REST API scripts using \texttt{yfinance}. To maintain empirical consistency across international exchanges and global cryptocurrency markets, all daily series were aligned to the primary trading calendar of the SPDR S\&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY).

Daily log returns were computed using the continuous compounding formulation:

\begin{equation}
  r_{i,t} = \ln\left(\frac{P_{i,t}}{P_{i,t-1}}\right)
\end{equation}

where $P_{i,t}$ represents the adjusted closing price of asset $i$ on trading day $t$. Continuous log returns ensure additivity across temporal horizons and prevent asymmetrical compounding distortion during high-volatility regimes.
