\section{Data Acquisition and Preparation}

The construction of a quantitative multi asset portfolio requires empirical rigor during data collection, market calendar alignment, missing value interpolation, and feature engineering. This section details the complete end-to-end data pipeline executed for Group 3, tracing raw multi-source ingestion through final matrix transformations.

\subsection{Asset Universe and Ingestion Pipeline}

The portfolio universe spans seven financial assets across four distinct asset classes, alongside the benchmark index and domestic risk free rate. The sampling horizon spans January 2, 2020 through January 2, 2026 (the six calendar years 2020--2025 plus the first US trading day of 2026), yielding a master panel of 1,509 daily trading dates per asset.

Raw asset prices were programmatically ingested via automated Python REST scripts leveraging the \texttt{yfinance} API \cite{yahoo_finance} for US-listed and cryptocurrency instruments. Philippine equities are sourced from their primary PSE listings through the official PSE EDGE portal \cite{pse_edge}, then converted to US dollars to preserve a single currency denomination across the portfolio.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PLDT Inc. (TEL):} Primary PSE listing accessed via NYSE depositary ticker PHI.
  \item \textbf{Manila Electric Company (MER):} Primary PSE listing, daily OHLCV from PSE EDGE, converted from PHP to USD.
  \item \textbf{Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV):} Primary PSE listing, daily OHLCV from PSE EDGE, converted from PHP to USD.
  \item \textbf{NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA):} Ingested directly from NASDAQ exchange endpoints.
  \item \textbf{Meta Platforms, Inc. (META):} Ingested directly from NASDAQ exchange endpoints.
  \item \textbf{Bitcoin (BTC):} Ingested from global cryptocurrency spot market feeds as BTC-USD.
  \item \textbf{SPDR S\&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY):} Ingested from NYSE Arca as the benchmark ETF.
  \item \textbf{Risk-Free Rate:} Annualized Philippine 91-Day Treasury Bill secondary market rate (5.25 percent benchmark) \cite{bsp_tbill}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Calendar Alignment, Relational Joins, and Imputation}

Financial asset price series originate from heterogeneous trading environments operating on asynchronous market schedules. Cryptocurrency markets trade continuously 24 hours a day across 365 days a year, whereas US equity exchanges observe NYSE holiday schedules and Philippine equities trade on PSE operating calendars.

To reconcile calendar discrepancies, individual asset time series were joined using relational outer joins keyed on ISO-8601 standardized date timestamps. The master date index was anchored to the official 1,509 trading day calendar of the SPDR S\&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY).

Philippine closing prices from PSE EDGE are quoted in Philippine pesos and are converted to US dollars using the daily PHP per USD spot rate (WM/Refinitiv via the \texttt{USDPHP=X} feed). The rate observed on each PSE trading date is applied to that date's prices, with the most recent published rate carried forward when the FX feed does not publish on a PSE-only trading day. This keeps the entire portfolio, including the SPY benchmark, in a single currency.

Missing observations are handled explicitly by instrument type. For the US-listed TEL ADR, a zero-volume row from the vendor carries no new price signal and is stored as missing rather than carried forward; treating a stale quote as a settlement price would manufacture zero-return observations and distort variance, correlation, and tail-risk estimates. For the PSE-listed instruments, dates absent from the PSE trading calendar are naturally missing in the master panel. Beyond Philippine holidays falling on US trading days, these absences reflect genuine market closures over the sample: the two-day suspension of March 17--18, 2020; calamity-driven closures (Taal volcano ashfall on January 13, 2020, Typhoon Ulysses on November 12, 2020, Super Typhoon Karding on September 26, 2022, the suspension of July 24, 2024, and election-day closure on May 12, 2025); and a trading cancellation from a systems glitch on January 4, 2022. One further date, October 24, 2022, is missing for both names even though the PSE was open that day, reflecting a gap in the captured PSE EDGE records rather than a market closure; October 25, 2022 is missing for AEV alone, a per-stock no-trade day on which MER printed a single flat trade. No imputation is applied to the stored panel: missing rows remain missing, and the ingestion script reports per-asset traded-day counts and missing-day totals on every refresh. At the analysis layer, wealth aggregation and backtesting carry the last observed price across no-trade days so that the resumption-day return captures the full gap move, while descriptive statistics and correlations are computed on observed returns only; no-trade days are never assigned a zero return.

\subsection{Feature Engineering and Mathematical Transformations}

Raw adjusted closing prices were subjected to mathematical transformations to construct stationary return series, scaled benchmark features, cumulative growth paths, and risk parameters required for downstream quantitative modeling.

\subsubsection{Logarithmic Return Transformation}

Adjusted closing prices were transformed into continuously compounded daily log returns according to the standard logarithmic return formula.

\begin{equation}
  r_{i,t} = \ln\left(\frac{P_{i,t}}{P_{i,t-1}}\right) = \ln(P_{i,t}) - \ln(P_{i,t-1})
\end{equation}

Logarithmic transformation converts compounding price ratios into linear additive values over time, facilitating multi period horizon aggregation. Log transformation also stabilizes return variance across high growth regimes, ensuring that extreme price movements in assets like Bitcoin or NVIDIA remain scale comparable with lower-volatility equities.

\subsubsection{Risk-Free Rate Daily Scaling}

The domestic benchmark risk free rate is quoted as an annualized yield percentage of 5.25 percent per annum. To align the risk free rate with daily log return series, the annual yield was transformed into a daily log equivalent rate using a 252 trading day annual convention.

\begin{equation}
  r_{f,t} = \frac{\ln(1 + R_f^{\text{annual}})}{252} = \frac{\ln(1 + 0.0525)}{252} \approx 0.00020307
\end{equation}

This transformation yields a constant daily risk free baseline of 0.0203 percent per day, which serves as the hurdle rate in subsequent Sharpe ratio and excess return computations.

\subsubsection{Cumulative Wealth Index Feature}

To track total capital appreciation from an initial baseline of USD 1.00, a cumulative wealth index feature was constructed for each asset by exponentiating cumulative log returns.

\begin{equation}
  W_{i,t} = \exp\left( \sum_{\tau=1}^{t} r_{i,\tau} \right) = \prod_{\tau=1}^{t} \left( \frac{P_{i,\tau}}{P_{i,\tau-1}} \right)
\end{equation}

The cumulative wealth feature provides an intuitive trajectory of asset growth without distortion from daily volatility oscillations.

\subsubsection{Rolling Volatility and Covariance Engineering}

Dynamic risk features were generated by computing 30-day and 60-day rolling annualized standard deviations and rolling pairwise covariance matrices.

\begin{equation}
  \sigma_{i,t}^{\text{annual}} = \sqrt{252 \times \frac{1}{N-1} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} (r_{i,t-k} - \bar{r}_{i,t})^2 }
\end{equation}

These engineered features capture time varying volatility regimes, enabling conditional risk evaluation during crisis periods such as the March 2020 market crash and 2022 rate hike cycles.
