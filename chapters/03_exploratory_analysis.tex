\section{Exploratory Financial Analysis}

Exploratory financial analysis establishes the empirical foundation required to construct robust portfolio optimization algorithms and risk management frameworks. This section provides an exhaustive evaluation of central tendency, volatility dynamics, distributional shape, tail risk diagnostics, and pairwise asset return co-movements across the 1,508 daily log return observations spanning 2020 through 2025.

\subsection{Empirical Descriptive Statistics and Risk Return Profiles}

Table~\ref{tab:desc_stats} summarizes the foundational descriptive statistics computed from the daily log return series across all seven portfolio assets.

\input{tables/tbl_desc_stats.tex}

The empirical results reveal pronounced divergence in expected return and risk parameters across asset classes. NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) generated the highest average daily log return at 0.0023, translating to an annualized return exceeding 57.9 percent. Bitcoin (BTC) followed with a daily log return mean of 0.0017, representing an annualized performance of 42.8 percent. Meta Platforms (META) and the benchmark SPDR S\&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY) recorded moderate positive daily return averages of 0.0008 and 0.0006 respectively. Domestic Philippine equities displayed subdued central tendencies, with Manila Electric Company (MER) producing 0.0005 daily and PLDT Inc. (TEL) generating 0.0003 daily. Aboitiz Equity Ventures (AEV) stood as the sole asset with a negative daily mean return of -0.0005 over the six year window.

Daily volatility metrics highlight severe dispersion in asset risk exposure. Bitcoin exhibited the highest daily standard deviation of 0.0393, representing an annualized volatility of 62.4 percent. NVIDIA recorded daily volatility of 0.0333, while Meta Platforms and Aboitiz Equity Ventures both registered daily standard deviations of 0.0279. Domestic utility equities PLDT and Manila Electric demonstrated defensive stability with lower daily standard deviations of 0.0199 and 0.0193 respectively. The benchmark SPY displayed the lowest daily return volatility across the universe at 0.0131, reflecting the structural risk reduction achieved through broad market diversification.

Third and fourth statistical moments demonstrate severe departure from Gaussian normality across all assets. Skewness measures reveal substantial left tail risk for Aboitiz Equity Ventures at -3.3581, PLDT at -1.7687, Bitcoin at -1.2226, and Meta Platforms at -1.0731. These negative skewness values confirm that extreme negative drawdowns occur with greater magnitude than positive price surges. Conversely, Manila Electric Company produced a positive skewness of 2.0662, reflecting infrequent but substantial upward price re-rating jumps. NVIDIA exhibited near zero skewness at 0.0434, indicating balanced directional return dispersion despite high total volatility.

Kurtosis metrics provide decisive evidence of fat tailed leptokurtosis across the entire asset universe. Theoretical Gaussian normal distributions possess a standard kurtosis of 3.0. Aboitiz Equity Ventures recorded an extreme kurtosis value of 109.4985, driven by catastrophic single day price declines during emerging market sell-offs. Manila Electric Company registered a kurtosis of 83.2401, while PLDT recorded 31.4844. Meta Platforms, Bitcoin, and SPY demonstrated elevated kurtosis values of 22.0943, 16.2966, and 13.3999 respectively. Even NVIDIA, the least leptokurtic asset in the universe, recorded a kurtosis of 4.0565. These excess kurtosis figures prove that asset returns contain heavy tail shocks that render standard mean variance models inadequate without explicit fat tail risk adjustments.

The time series return trajectories exhibit prominent autoregressive conditional heteroskedasticity, commonly known as volatility clustering. The 2022 global monetary tightening cycle produced sustained return dispersion in Meta Platforms and NVIDIA, characterized by sharp single day drawdowns following quarterly earnings announcements. Philippine domestic equities displayed isolated volatility bursts corresponding to local macroeconomic policy shifts and corporate restructuring announcements, operating largely independently of global tech sector turbulence.

\subsection{Distributional Shape and Non-Normality Diagnostics}

Figure~\ref{fig:density_hist} compares the empirical kernel density distributions of daily log returns against fitted Gaussian normal distribution curves.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \safeincludegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/fig-density-hist-1.pdf}
  \caption{Empirical Return Distributions vs. Gaussian Reference Curves}
  \label{fig:density_hist}
\end{figure}

The empirical density overlays highlight two distinct structural departures from classical normal distributions. First, the central peaks of the empirical distributions rise significantly higher than theoretical normal curves, demonstrating a high concentration of small daily price movements around the median. Second, the shoulders of the empirical distributions collapse faster than normal curves, while the extreme tails extend much farther outward. This structural shape confirms that asset returns experience long periods of low volatility punctuated by sudden, severe market shocks.

Figure~\ref{fig:qq_plots} provides normal Quantile-Quantile (Q-Q) diagnostic plots to evaluate tail behavior against theoretical Gaussian quantiles.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \safeincludegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/fig-qq-plots-1.pdf}
  \caption{Normal Q-Q Diagnostics Demonstrating Heavy Tail Leptokurtosis}
  \label{fig:qq_plots}
\end{figure}

The normal Q-Q plots display pronounced S-shaped departures from the red dashed 45-degree theoretical reference line across every single asset. In the lower left quadrant, sample quantiles curve sharply downward below the theoretical line, confirming that negative market drawdowns are vastly larger than predicted by normal distribution assumptions. In the upper right quadrant, sample quantiles curve upward above the theoretical line, reflecting severe upside price spikes. The severity of tail curvature is most extreme for Aboitiz Equity Ventures, Manila Electric Company, and Bitcoin, proving that risk models assuming Gaussian normality will severely underestimate tail risk and Value at Risk thresholds.

Domestic Philippine equities (TEL, MER, AEV) exhibit narrow interquartile ranges bounded between -0.0100 and +0.0100 daily log returns, but display extreme individual outlier points reaching -0.4724 for AEV and +0.2868 for MER.

These dispersion characteristics indicate that while domestic equities offer day to day stability, international technology and crypto assets require active risk management to mitigate severe tail event exposure.

Combining high return, high volatility international tech assets with uncorrelated domestic Philippine equities enables substantial portfolio variance reduction without sacrificing long term expected return.
