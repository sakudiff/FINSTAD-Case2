\section{Exploratory Financial Analysis}

This section presents empirical descriptive statistics, distributional shape diagnostics, return dispersion comparisons, and correlation structures across the asset universe over the 2020 through 2025 sampling period.

\subsection{Descriptive Statistical Summary}

Table~\ref{tab:desc_stats} provides the summary statistics computed from daily log returns across all portfolio assets and the benchmark index.

\input{tables/tbl_desc_stats.tex}

The descriptive results highlight clear risk-return trade-offs. International technology equities and cryptocurrency delivered higher average daily log returns, accompanied by substantially elevated volatility. Bitcoin (BTC) exhibited the highest daily standard deviation, followed by NVIDIA (NVDA) and Meta Platforms (META). Conversely, Philippine equities (TEL, MER, AEV) demonstrated lower volatility profiles, functioning as defensive anchors within the portfolio.

All asset return distributions exhibit excess kurtosis (kurtosis greater than 3.0), indicating leptokurtic behavior characterized by fat tails and heightened frequency of extreme market movements relative to a normal distribution.
