\section{Portfolio Risk Assessment}

To evaluate downside vulnerability beyond standard deviation, this section computes Historical and Parametric Value at Risk (VaR), Expected Shortfall (CVaR), Monte Carlo simulated return distributions, and historical crisis stress tests.

\subsection{Value at Risk (VaR) and Expected Shortfall (CVaR)}

Value at Risk ($\text{VaR}_\alpha$) represents the maximum expected loss at confidence level $\alpha$ over a daily horizon. Expected Shortfall ($\text{ES}_\alpha$ or CVaR) measures the expected loss conditional on exceeding the VaR threshold.

Table~\ref{tab:var_summary} reports daily 95 percent and 99 percent VaR and Expected Shortfall metrics for both the GMV and Maximum Sharpe portfolios.

\input{tables/tbl_var_summary.tex}

For the GMV portfolio, the daily 95 percent Historical VaR is 1.32 percent, with an Expected Shortfall of 2.14 percent. The Maximum Sharpe portfolio incurs significantly higher tail risk, recording a 95 percent Historical VaR of 2.61 percent and a 95 percent Expected Shortfall of 4.12 percent (reaching 6.25 percent at the 99 percent confidence level).
